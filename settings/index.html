Click on a result

Right-click a result for the other choice. Placing never overwrites clips. It uses the first free track at the playhead.

Save files

"Next to the project" makes a "Zen Stickers" folder beside the project file. An unsaved project uses the folder below.

Empty search box

"Show nothing" keeps the panel quiet until you type. Each tab still has a "Show trending" link.

Content rating

Download GIFs and stickers as

GIF: works everywhere, keeps see-through parts. MP4: smaller and smoother, no see-through parts (stickers stay GIF). WebM: small and keeps see-through parts, when the source has it (KLIPY). If the app cannot read a WebM, the GIF is used instead.

Loop short clips

A short clip becomes one clip that repeats. Premiere: ffmpeg makes a looped copy (ProRes 4444, transparency kept) and it goes on a free track. After Effects: the footage's own loop setting.

Loop markers

"Where the loop starts" is the end of the first pass, where the clip begins to repeat.

ffmpeg

Looking for ffmpeg…

On Windows, Zen Stickers downloads ffmpeg by itself (about 100 MB, once) the first time a loop needs it. It comes from github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds, the LGPL build, checked against its published checksum.

Your own keys: free, and only saved on this computer. Everything else works without a key: Google Images (it opens in your browser), Wikimedia, Openverse, Twitch emotes, 7TV, FrankerFaceZ, emoji.gg, Iconify and all emojis.

Premiere gets a PNG this big. After Effects gets SVG where the design has one, and a PNG this big where it does not.

AI upscale

Apple, Samsung, WhatsApp and Facebook are only 108 to 512 px. The upscaler (Real-ESRGAN) runs on your graphics card, about 2 s per new emoji. Each emoji is upscaled once, then kept in the cache.
